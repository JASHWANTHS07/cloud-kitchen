Our story

Started at home. Still cooked like it.

Saffron & Sage began with family recipes and a simple idea: food from a delivery kitchen should taste as honest as food from a home kitchen.

We keep the menu small so every dish gets our full attention. The masalas are ground in-house, the dal simmers overnight, and nothing is cooked before you order.

— The Saffron & Sage family

Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will reply.

How do I pay for a pickup order?

Pay by UPI or cash when you collect your food. For large orders we may ask for an advance on WhatsApp.

How long does a pickup order take?

Most orders are ready in about 30 minutes. You can choose a later pickup time when you place your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through Swiggy and Zomato. Use the buttons on this page to open our restaurant page in the app.

Can you make it less spicy, or Jain?

Most dishes can be made mild, and many can be prepared without onion and garlic. Add a note to your order and we'll confirm on WhatsApp.

Do you take bulk or party orders?

Yes. Message us at least a day in advance with the number of guests and we'll suggest a menu.
